Design practice experiences for your learners

Primer interviews you about your course, your learners and your materials. It then proposes a learning and simulation design for you to review, revise and approve. Once you approve it, Primer builds a playable experience in three connected phases: Preparation, Practice, and Feedback & reflection.

How it works

  1. Interview. Primer asks one question at a time and suggests key ideas from any materials you upload.
  2. Design review. Every part of the design shows where it came from: from you, from your materials, or an AI suggestion for you to check. You can edit any part directly or ask for a revision in plain language.
  3. Approval. Nothing reaches learners until you approve it.
  4. Learners play. The learner does the thinking. Primer adapts its support and challenge but never hands over the answer.
  5. Evidence. Feedback quotes what the learner actually said, and you can trace every judgment back to your input.
