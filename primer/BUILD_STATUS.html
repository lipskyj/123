What works now

  • Teacher intake, one-question-at-a-time interview, proposed design with sources, assumptions and suggestions
  • Direct edits and natural-language revision, each creating a new version; downstream prompts and scenes regenerate
  • Approval states (draft, revised, test-approved, educator-approved, rejected, superseded) enforced at build and at session start
  • Student runtime: Preparation, Practice (Roleplay or Teachback, optional 3D cave field work), Feedback with self-assessment, citations and transfer
  • Adaptation tied to criteria and evidence; answer-seeking declined; misconceptions probed
  • Persistence and resume; export / import; teacher view of sessions, evidence and adaptations
