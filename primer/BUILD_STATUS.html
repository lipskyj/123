What works now

  • Teacher intake, one-question-at-a-time interview, proposed design with visible sources, assumptions and suggestions
  • Direct edits and plain-language revisions; versions, restore, superseding; every fixed character line visible before approval
  • Approval states enforced at build, start and during sessions; test approval labelled everywhere; withdrawal and replacement
  • Student copy carries no private teacher text
  • Preparation with full material, Practice (Roleplay/Teachback, case facts, optional 3D cave field notes), Feedback with self-assessment, quotations, 'not assessed' and transfer
  • Answer requests always declined; criteria never answered are not graded
  • Session records with evidence and adaptations visible to the teacher; export/import
