The head-office overhead allocation is a real cost of the project — only include overhead if total company overhead actually changes because of the project
Interest on the equipment loan should be subtracted from the cash flows — financing is reflected in the discount rate, so subtracting interest double-counts it
Working capital is just an expense that disappears — it is an investment at launch that is recovered when the line ends, so it is a timing cost
