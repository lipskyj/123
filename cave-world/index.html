Your kit

    How it works

    Find something interesting, pick the right tool, then carry out the procedure yourself and interpret what you see. Each find earns up to three stars: done, clean technique, right interpretation first time. Using the wrong tool on something fragile damages the site, and that goes in your report. When you have enough evidence, press Report to make your recommendation.

    Keep your phone on. Dr. Reyes will call, sometimes when something is going wrong and you have seconds to act. You can call her too (the Call button, or C): she can nudge you, but she logs every hint.
